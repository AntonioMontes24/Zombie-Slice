











The town of Crimson was quiet once.

Until the dead started to walk.

No warning. No escape.

Just blood, screams... and silence.

Now, the streets are crawling with them.

Hungry. Relentless. Unstoppable.

I don’t know why I’m still alive.

Maybe luck. Maybe a curse.

But I’m not waiting to find out.

Something’s calling from beneath the city.

The old crypt—they say it was sealed for a reason.

Now it’s open. And they’re pouring out.

Whatever started this is down there.

And I’m going in.

One clip left. One way out.

This ends tonight.
