




It’s over.
The thing that controlled them… dead.
Its bodyguards, gone.
Whatever it was—ancient, unnatural—it bled like anything else.
But Crimson is silent now.
Too silent.
No cheers. No rescue. No one left.

Just smoke...
...and the stench of rot.

I made it out. Barely.
But survival isn’t victory.
Not when the city is ash.
Not when the dead still whisper in the dark.

I saw something before it died.
A mark... carved in its flesh.
It wasn’t the beginning.
It was a gate.

Crimson fell... but this plague—
It’s not buried.
It’s waiting.

And I’m not done yet.
